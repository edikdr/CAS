Two guitars given at Christmas, a warehouse in Neepsend, a stack of burned CDs that escaped onto the internet before the band had anything to sell — and then twenty years of refusing to make the same record twice.

The same four people on stage since 2006, and the bassist who played on the record that started it. Open any card for the full profile.

Every record is printed from its own data — no images, just paper. Press PLAY to uncrumple one; press any line to open the track.

The ones that arrived on their own.

An unofficial, non-commercial fan archive. Not affiliated with Arctic Monkeys, Domino Recording Company, or anyone who works with them.

Where the information comes from. Track listings, durations and credits are the released studio versions. Songwriter and producer credits, and the complete words to every song, live on Genius — every track window links straight to its page there, and the site can pull credits from the Genius API directly. Preview audio and artwork come from the iTunes Search API, with Deezer as a second source; release data is cross-checked against MusicBrainz. All four are free and used within their public terms.

On the words. The lines in each track window are short signature excerpts with approximate fan timings, for identification and study. Full lyrics are not reproduced here — they are Genius's to show, and every window says so and links there.

How it is built. Vanilla HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No framework, no build step, no tracking. The receipts, the barcodes, the waveform and every cut-out in the background are drawn in the browser rather than shipped as images — the whole page is a few hundred kilobytes. The only pictures are 52 scans of cut magazine letters, shared with the sister site in this repository.
