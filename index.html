The Band

Cigarettes After Sex formed in 2008 in El Paso, Texas, around songwriter Greg Gonzalez. The name came from a private moment — intimacy, then the quiet ritual afterwards.

The earliest recordings happened inside a four-story stairwell at the University of Texas at El Paso. That building is the reverb you hear on the I. EP (2012), and on “Nothing’s Gonna Hurt You Baby”, a song that sat almost unheard until recommendation algorithms found it years later.

After a move to Brooklyn came the single “Affection” (2015) and then the self-titled debut in 2017 on Partisan Records. Hushed androgynous vocals, endless reverb, lyrics about wanting people — it became a global mood. Cry followed in 2019, written in a villa on Mallorca. X’s arrived in 2024.

Current core: Greg Gonzalez (vocals, guitar), Randall Miller (bass), Jacob Tomsky (drums).
