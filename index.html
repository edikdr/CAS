Quizzes

Fifty-six questions across four decks. An answer locks once chosen and the right one is always shown, so a wrong guess still tells you something — and the four options are re-shuffled every play, so there is no pattern to learn. Answer with the mouse or press 1–4.

An unofficial fan archive. Not affiliated with Hollywood Undead, with BMG, or with anyone who works with them.

Listings and previews: iTunes Search API. Words: LRCLIB, fetched on demand and stored nowhere. Credits, annotations and release data: Genius, when a token is set in this deployment's environment. Every track panel says which of those actually answered.
